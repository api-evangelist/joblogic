Awaab's Law Phase 2 brings seven new hazard categories under the same statutory deadlines that have applied to damp and mould since October 2025. What changes with Phase 2 isn't the deadlines themselves, it's the range of jobs a repairs team now has to recognise, triage, and evidence against them.
